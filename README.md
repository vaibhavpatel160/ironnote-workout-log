# IronNote — Lightweight Mobile Workout Notebook

**Live App (Phone & Desktop):** [https://vaibhavpatel160.github.io/ironnote-workout-log/](https://vaibhavpatel160.github.io/ironnote-workout-log/)

A fast, zero-bloat Progressive Web App (PWA) inspired by a ruled gym notebook (`Exercise | Weight | Reps | Sets`), with **multi-set variable weights & reps**, **end-of-week volume tracking**, and an **evidence-based Weekly Hardness Estimate (`0–10`)**.

## Use on Your Phone (Anywhere, Online or Offline)
1. Open **[https://vaibhavpatel160.github.io/ironnote-workout-log/](https://vaibhavpatel160.github.io/ironnote-workout-log/)** on your phone.
2. **iPhone (Safari)**: Tap **Share → Add to Home Screen** for full-screen native-app feel and offline gym access.
3. **Android (Chrome)**: Tap **Menu → Install App / Add to Home Screen**.
4. All workouts save privately in your phone's browser storage (`localStorage`), with 1-tap **JSON & CSV export/import** under **Lifts & Data**.

## Features
- **Different Weights & Reps for the Same Exercise**: Tap **`+ Diff Weight / Reps`** inside any exercise to log pyramid sets, top sets + back-off sets, or drop sets (e.g. `185 lb × 10 × 1`, `205 lb × 8 × 2`, `225 lb × 5 × 1`, or weighted pull-ups `bw + 45 lb × 4` followed by `bw × 10`).
- **Notebook-Speed Logging**: Supports Push / Pull / Leg / Upper / Lower splits, 1-tap **"Autofill Last [Split]"**, quick exercise chips, and notebook shorthand weights (`200`, `bw`, `bw + 45 lb`, or `×2 DB` dumbbell pairs).
- **End-of-Week Volume**: Tracks total weekly poundage (`lb`), external plate load vs. bodyweight load, working sets, week-over-week progression (`vs last week`), daily Mon–Sun bars, and split balance.
- **Weekly Hardness Estimate (`0–10`)**: Combines weekly session consistency, working set volume landmarks, bodyweight-normalized load & heavy strength sets (`1–6 reps`), and session RPE into a clear score + recovery takeaway.
